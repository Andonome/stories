\mapentry[jokeTime]{You tell the jester}
``\textit{my dog has no nose}'' and the jester asks, taking the bait with
``\textit{How does he smell?}''.

``\textit{Just like you!}'', and the crowd giggle.
``\textit{You always have the same clothes, maybe wash them once a month?}'', you say, holding your nose.

Even the \glspl{sunGuard} give a chuckle.
But can you keep it up?
